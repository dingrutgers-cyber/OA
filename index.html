Why is Friday missing? Friday is the reference category. With an intercept, including all seven weekday dummy variables creates perfect multicollinearity because the seven indicators sum to 1. Omitting one weekday avoids the dummy-variable trap. Changing the reference weekday changes coefficient interpretation, not equivalent fitted values.
